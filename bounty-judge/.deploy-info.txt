--- Why Ritual Chain (not Base) ---
The commit-reveal logic runs on any EVM chain, but judgeAll() calls the LLM
inference precompile at 0x0802. That precompile only exists on Ritual Chain
(chainId 1979), so the full lifecycle (including AI judging + finalizeWinner)
requires deploying to Ritual — a Base mainnet deployment could not run judgeAll.

--- Ritual ms-timestamp note ---
Ritual reports block.timestamp in MILLISECONDS (~1.78e12). The contract
auto-detects this (block.timestamp > 1e12) and normalises to seconds via
_now(), so callers always pass standard second-based deadlines and the
contract works unchanged on any EVM chain. See RitualMsTimestampTest.
